# The Financial Wisdom – Editorial DNA

## Mission

Explain finance and economics so clearly that a motivated 15-year-old can understand them without prior knowledge.

---

## Audience

Primary:
- Students
- Young professionals
- Retail investors
- Curious learners

Knowledge Level:
Beginner

Reading Level:
Grade 8

---

## Core Principles

Every explanation must answer:

1. What is it?
2. Why does it exist?
3. Why should I care?
4. How does it affect my money or life?
5. What happens next?

---

## Writing Style

- Simple English
- Short sentences
- No unnecessary jargon
- Explain every technical term immediately
- Never assume prior knowledge

---

## Storytelling Rules

Every script should contain:

- One hook
- One relatable story
- One analogy
- One real-world example
- One surprising fact
- One recap

---

## Things to Avoid

- Buzzwords
- Complex academic language
- Long paragraphs
- Unexplained acronyms
- Fear-mongering
- Clickbait that the video cannot justify

---

## Tone

Friendly

Curious

Objective

Trustworthy

Optimistic

Evidence-based

---

## Goal

The viewer should finish the video saying:

"I finally understand this."
